What is this project?
A ready-to-use personal portfolio template to showcase your work and skills.

Key Features:
Works on all devices (computer, tablet, mobile)

Modern and clean design

Fast performance - no heavy libraries

Easy to customize colors and content

Main Sections:
Homepage - Your photo and introduction

About Section - Personal info and skills

Services - Services you offer

Portfolio - Your previous projects

Statistics - Your achievements and numbers

Testimonials - Client reviews

Blog - Your articles or news

Contact - Contact form

How to use it?
Download the files

Open index.html in any browser

Edit texts and images to fit your needs

Upload to hosting to make it your personal website

For Developers:
Well-organized and easy-to-understand code

Easy to add new features

Supports all modern browsers
